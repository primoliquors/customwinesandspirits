Best Wine & Spirits Shops in Tampa Bay (2026)

Tampa Bay’s independent scene runs from a 1960s allocated-bourbon “collector’s den” to a Wine Enthusiast–listed bottle shop and a cluster of St. Pete boutique wine markets.

Researched and published by the team at Primo Liquors. We cover shops across Florida on their merits and feature Primo where we genuinely believe it’s a standout.

Temple Terrace / Tampa · Yelp 4.5★

Family-owned since 1960, with Tampa’s largest selection of allocated and craft bourbon plus single-malt scotch — a genuine collector’s den.
