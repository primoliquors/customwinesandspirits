Getting Spirits Shipped to Your Door

You don't have to live near a great bottle shop to buy from one. Licensed retailers ship wine and spirits to most U.S. states — with an adult-signature delivery at your door. Here's how it works, what to look for, and a state-by-state list for the shop we know best.

How buying spirits online actually works

  1. You order from a licensed retailer. The store you buy from should be a real, licensed brick-and-mortar shop — not a marketplace middleman. Real stores have addresses, phone numbers that answer, and a published shipping policy.
  2. The retailer ships where it's permitted to. Every legitimate shop publishes the list of states it ships to; if your state isn't on the list, the checkout won't take your address.
  3. An adult signs on delivery. Carriers require someone 21+ with ID to sign — packages are never left at the door. Ship to an address where an adult will be around, or use a carrier pickup point.

Rule of thumb: if a rare bottle from an unknown seller is priced too good to be true, it is. Buy rare bottles from shops with a storefront you could walk into.

FAQ

Can I get spirits shipped to my state?

It depends on the retailer and your state. Primo Liquors ships to 44 states — every state except Michigan, Alaska, Arkansas, Mississippi, Utah, and Hawaii. Other retailers publish their own lists; the checkout enforces it either way.

How does alcohol delivery work when it arrives?

Carriers require an adult signature: someone 21 or older must be present and show ID. Packages can't be left at the door, so ship to an address where an adult will be available — home, office, or a carrier pickup point.

What should I look for when buying spirits online?

A licensed brick-and-mortar retailer with real store locations, secure checkout, a published shipping policy, and a phone number that answers. Avoid marketplace sellers with no storefront, especially for rare bottles.

Is local delivery cheaper than shipping?

If you're in the retailer's area, usually yes. In Broward County, Primo Liquors delivers free on orders over $150.
