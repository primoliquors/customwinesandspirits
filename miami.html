Best Wine & Spirits Shops in Miami-Dade (2026)

Miami-Dade’s best bottle shops are independents with a real point of view — fine-and-rare specialists, natural-wine rooms, and a rare-bourbon collector scene — not the chains. Here’s where locals and collectors actually shop.

Researched and published by the team at Primo Liquors. We cover shops across Florida on their merits and feature Primo where we genuinely believe it’s a standout.

Finding rare bottles in Miami-Dade

For allocated bourbon and aged tequila, Rare Collects and Sunset Corners are the collector stops; for fine and back-vintage wine, Vinonueva and 305 Wines. Ask about tasting schedules — Miami shops lean heavily on in-store tastings.

Chains (for context, not independents): ABC Fine Wine & Spirits and Total Wine dominate the metro.
