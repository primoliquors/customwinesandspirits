Best Wine & Spirits Shops in Jacksonville & North Florida (2026)

Jacksonville and St. Augustine reward whiskey hunters and wine lovers alike, with family-owned bourbon specialists and boutique wine shops with real hospitality.

Researched and published by the team at Primo Liquors. We cover shops across Florida on their merits and feature Primo where we genuinely believe it’s a standout.

Featured shops

Independent wine and spirits shops in Jacksonville & North Florida, chosen for selection, service and fair pricing. Confirm current details with the shop before a trip.

Shores Fine Wine & Spirits

Jacksonville / Jax Beach

Family-owned, with deep allocated and rare bourbon and whiskey, fine wine and strong service; multiple locations and a drive-thru.

shoresliquor.com ↗

Mark’s Liquor

Jacksonville (University Blvd)

Consistently cited as Jax’s top whiskey and bourbon store, with a loyalty program that gates access to rare and allocated bottles.

The Grape & Grain Exchange

Jacksonville (San Marco)

A boutique bottle shop with an attached cocktail parlour, curated small-batch wine and spirits, cheese, tastings and education.

grapeandgrainexchange.com ↗

The Gifted Cork

St. Augustine

Fine wine from around the world, gourmet cheese and gifts, and daily tastings with owner-led hospitality.

thegiftedcork.com ↗

Carrera Wine Cellar

St. Augustine

Retail wine (many under $20) with a tasting bar, craft beer and sommelier-led classes.

Broudy’s Fine Wine & Spirits

Jacksonville & St. Augustine

A 70-year northeast-Florida institution with five stores between Jacksonville and St. Augustine; spirits-focused with a deep selection.

broudys.com ↗

Finding rare bottles in Jacksonville & North Florida
