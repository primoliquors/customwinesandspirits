Best Wine & Spirits Shops in Palm Beach County (2026)

From a Master Sommelier’s Palm Beach shop to a Boca “largest independent” and a West Palm natural-wine specialist, Palm Beach County’s independents punch well above the chains for service and rare sourcing.

Researched and published by the team at Primo Liquors. We cover shops across Florida on their merits and feature Primo where we genuinely believe it’s a standout.

Featured shops

Independent wine and spirits shops in Palm Beach County, chosen for selection, service and fair pricing. Confirm current details with the shop before a trip.

Virginia Philip Wine, Spirits & Academy

Palm Beach · Google 4.8★

Founded by Master Sommelier Virginia Philip (former Breakers wine director); retail plus a wine-education academy, deep Old- and New-World selections, sake and gourmet.

“Virginia Phillips and her staff are first class all the way, and as a Master Som, she is second to none!!!!” — Google review

4S Liquors

Boca Raton · Google 4.7★

Bills itself as Florida’s largest independent liquor store — 2,500+ curated wines, spirits and craft beer, strong bourbon and Japanese whisky, and concierge sourcing of rare and allocated bottles.

“The owners are truly caring everything is fairly priced” — Google review

4sliquors.com ↗

West Palm Wine Co.

West Palm Beach

A true mom-and-pop with ~700 small-grower organic, biodynamic and natural wines; founder Michael Drapkin came from Kingston Wine Co. and Balthazar NYC.

“The BEST wine shop in WPB! If you're looking for a curated selection of small batch, natural wines, this is THE spot.” — Yelp review

The Wine Wave

Delray Beach (Atlantic Ave) · Tripadvisor 4.8★

A luxury boutique of small-production, natural and biodynamic wine, allocated bottles and fine Champagne.

“The selection and pricing is great, but the service and knowledge of the folks at the Wine Wave is the selling point” — Tripadvisor review

Hamptons Palm Beach · verify

Palm Beach · Google 4.9★

Provenance-driven fine wine — First Growth Bordeaux, rare Burgundy, Super Tuscans — plus cigars and caviar; manages estate and yacht cellars.

“If you're looking for a wine and spirits shop in Palm Beach that feels personal, a little upscale, and still totally down-to-earth, this is it.” — Google review
